# Onixus brand system v2

Approved direction: metallic Core mark, three outer pillars and one hot-green center, on jet black. Retain purple at top, magenta left, cyan right, and platinum ONIXUS lettering.

## Naming
Onixus Workspace is the umbrella. Onixus Mail, Onixus CRM, and Onixus Signal are products. Use distinct product marks with a shared brushed-metal surface and platinum typography. Workspace keeps the Core; Mail uses cyan envelope, CRM purple relationship nodes, Signal the approved multicolor waveform, Chat green/cyan dialogue frame with smooth inner corner. Remove legacy Veltra branding in a separately reviewed code change, never with a blind identifier replacement.

## Usage
Use the exact approved black-background PNG as the primary master. Keep generous clear space (at least 15% of mark width). Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add outer glow. Use metallic treatment for hero graphics; use restrained flat companion colors in UI. No rainbow gradients. Keep the metallic mark large enough for the texture to read; compact app icons and monochrome adaptations require a separate vector master.

The transparent PNG is an AI-generated secondary cutout and needs edge cleanup before light-background or print use. Primary black artwork is the approved reference. SVG templates embed the raster master: they have editable text/layout but are NOT true vector logo masters. Professional vector reconstruction and print proofing remain necessary before signage or large-format production.

## Palette and typography
Purple #9254FF; cyan #00CBE8; magenta #E625A5; hot green #00D67A; jet black #000000; platinum #EEF0F4. These flat companion tokens do not represent all metallic reflections. Use Arial/Helvetica sans-serif for immediately portable templates. Preserve the custom ONIXUS letterforms in the supplied artwork.

## Voice
Direct, confident, useful. Lead with the work the customer can accomplish. Main message: “Your business. Connected.” Supporting message: “Your conversations, customers, and next move—in one workspace.” Never claim zero manual entry, guaranteed revenue, universal integrations, or autonomous actions until verified in production.
